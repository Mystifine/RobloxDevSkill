# UI: faded backgrounds and shine

- **Identify color shading versus an opacity fade before building.** A shade behind a stat or text row that disappears into the game view needs a [UIGradient](https://create.roblox.com/docs/reference/engine/classes/UIGradient) with a varying `Transparency` `NumberSequence`. A uniformly translucent rectangle has a hard edge and does not reproduce that fade; changing `Color` alone does not fade to transparency.
- Put the gradient on a separate background Frame behind the icon and text, so the content stays fully visible. Set the Frame's base `BackgroundTransparency` low enough for the shade to show, then use gradient transparency keypoints to fade from the intended opacity to `1` at the disappearing edge. Match the fade's direction and extent with `Rotation`, `Offset` and keypoint positions. Keep `Color` constant for a pure opacity fade. In Studio-only work, author these instances and properties directly; no runtime script is needed.
- **Shine stays inside the face, clear of its strokes.** Frames are acceptable for shine bands, but size and inset their visible area from the innermost edge of every border/rim, including its thickness, offset, rounded corners and device scale. Keep the border continuous and unobscured. Match the reference's clearance; otherwise keep the normal 2 px interior margin. Rotating or animating a band must not carry its corners across the rim.
- Use a separate inset face for gloss and textures, with the outline drawn above it. Verify the actual rendered mask: a parent's `UICorner` or rectangular `ClipsDescendants` alone is not proof that a rotated child is contained by the rounded face. Use inset geometry or a suitable masked image when needed. A higher border `ZIndex` alone does not fix shine that extends outside the face.
- Only let shine cross a stroke when that overlap is explicitly present in the reference or requested design. Record that specific exception; do not infer it merely because the effect is decorative.
- **A hover glint inside a rounded face:** a white frame the size and corner of the plate's inset face, under a `UIGradient` whose transparency shows one narrow diagonal band, tweened from `Offset` -1 to 1 on `MouseEnter`. It stays inside the rounded face with no clipping, over the icon and under the caption: recorded over 58 frames inside a 104.9 px button (a 97.8 px glint).
- **A sunburst behind an offer or a ready button is a bold wedge texture:** a stock thin-rays "transparent sunburst" image on a bright rainbow card read as nothing at 0.45 transparency and barely at full opacity. An original 16-wedge white sunburst with a rim fade (drawn with Pillow, uploaded through Studio MCP) read clearly at 45% opacity, in two layers turning against each other, the front one breathing. **Size it well past what it sits behind and check it over the busiest backdrop it crosses:** rays 132 design units across behind a 90 x 82 icon on a 130-wide card, front at 0.55 and back at 0.8 transparency, were nearly invisible over a portal's purple; at 180 units, 0.3 and 0.55, two layers turning against each other (a 9 s turn, an 18% breath) and drawn under the card above (the card's `ZIndex` below its siblings) read clearly. **Then stop just past readable** *(default)*: that owner asked for them more transparent and tame, and they settled at 156 units, 0.62 and 0.8, an 18 s turn and a 5% breath. A glow behind a HUD offer stays soft and slow: about 0.6 transparency or more, a turn of 15 s or longer, a breath under 10%.
- **Fade an image in from `ImageTransparency` 0.99, never 1:** a loading screen's ImageLabel created at 1, to fade in once loaded, never loaded: `IsLoaded` stayed false for over 2 s and the icon never appeared. The same rbxthumb image at 0 loaded in 0.42 s, and the stuck label set to 0.99 loaded in 0.05 s, after which the fade ran. A fully transparent image is never fetched: start at 0.99, or preload it.
- **A prestige frame** *(default)*: a slow ramp of related hues, not the full spectrum. A rainbow on a top tier read as cheap.
- **A rare variant of a card or item** (shiny, gilded, holo) *(default)* shows in its frame, a tag and a sweep, never in the art: a translucent colour wash over the art read as faded and hid a gold variant, where a recoloured, spinning frame, an inner glow line and a strong foil sweep read at a glance.
- **An animated border with no images:** a dark inner `UIStroke`, a coloured one with a rotating `UIGradient`, and a white one with a moving transparency band, in one shared loop that writes rotations and offsets only, so transparency fades still work. It works on ScreenGui and SurfaceGui.
- **Mirror a picture** with a negative rect: `ImageRectOffset = (W, 0)`, `ImageRectSize = (-W, H)`. Two side by side, one mirrored (`ImageRectOffset` (1024, 0) and `ImageRectSize` (-1024, 683) on a 1024 x 683 picture), make a painted backdrop's edges meet themselves along a long wall.
