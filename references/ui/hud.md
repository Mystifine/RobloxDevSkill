# UI: HUD feedback, stats and notifications

What the HUD tells the player while they play: gains, stat readouts, notifications and calls to act, beside [ui.md](ui.md). A reward's effect landing before its payout: [runtime.md](runtime.md#state).

- **Feedback weight follows how often the event comes** *(default)*: no screen card for a frequent progress event, a card only for a rare milestone. An owner removed a full-screen level-up card because levels came too often and it got in the way more than it helped, and kept the card for evolutions, which are rare; the level-up's body effects and sound stayed.
- **A frequent gain shows where its counter is** *(default)*: each amount pops on screen and tweens into the counter's text, and a level-up is a small animated pop-up right over the total. A pop-up over the counter collided with the goal line above it until that line faded while the pop-up showed: fade whatever shares that space.
- **What multiplies a stat is listed beside it,** largest first, in a few short coloured lines *(default)*: an owner wanted the HUD's total centred over its bar with its multiplier sources (the progression tier, abilities, collectibles) on its left, as in their reference. Three lines of 16 design units drew at about 11 px at 1549 x 755; 19-unit lines read at about 14 px, with the per-swing line at 17 px. Every collectible tier is usually a source ([game-economy.md](../design/game-economy.md#economy-and-pacing)).
- **A ready progression step is called by its own button** *(default)*: the nav tile for the main progression step shows a white percentage toward the next one; at 100% the text runs an animated rainbow gradient, sunbursts turn behind the button ([surface.md](surface.md)) and the button shakes a little. The progress bar's own button acts at once, a one-tap step that skips opening the menu.
- **Notifications are plain outlined text stacked upward from just over the main stat** *(default)*: a TextLabel with no background at the bottom centre, each new line pushing the stack up from above the stat's total, replacing plate toasts at the top centre. The stack's foot follows the stat's live position, so a scale-only layout holds on every screen. The toast layer hides while a window is open: an action taken inside a window shows its result there ([windows.md](windows.md)). **Every transient notice joins that stack, never big text at the top** *(default)*: a currency count-up, a new area unlocked, a boss's arrival and a tutorial's end were big banners at the top of the screen that lingered, and an owner wanted them where the small toasts fade, asking a second time after a round had moved only some. When asked to move all of them, search every call site that writes text at the top of the screen.
- **On touch devices, buttons beat notifications** *(default)*: a dedicated toast ScreenGui with `DisplayOrder` -1 on touch (above the HUD elsewhere) put every button first in `PlayerGui:GetGuiObjectsAtPosition` (stack index 5 for a button against 12 for the toast); `ZIndex` inside one ScreenGui was fragile with nested layers. Check with that order.
- **Granted rewards show as picture cells** *(default)*: a dark full-screen layer over the top bar, a REWARDS heading, then one square cell per item with its own art (the pet's icon, the aura's emblem, the product's art, else the kind's icon), tap to close. A reward notice sent as text lines on a coloured card was replaced at an owner's request: the server's grant summary gained an item per line (kind, id, amount) so the client picks each picture, and a spec checks every reward kind yields an item with a picture. Their captions share one text size ([ui.md](ui.md), Engine gotchas).
- **The HUD carries stats, not guidance** *(default)*: no helper or hint text by the progress bar unless asked. An owner had a goal line and an auto-training banner over the progress bar removed, keeping the stat row they had asked for earlier; guidance belongs to onboarding ([onboarding.md](../design/onboarding.md)), not a standing line.
- **A countdown that decides a fight is big and central** *(default)*: digits near the top centre, about twice a corner timer's size, a draining bar, and red with a pulse each tick in its last 10 s. A 60 s challenge timer drawn as small text in a bar's corner went unnoticed; moved into the action button's slot under the bar, it needed no new layout room on any screen.
- **HUD offers:** at most two, the featured offer on top, rotating as they are owned ([offers.md](../design/offers.md)).
- **Fight lists keep out of the column above the player's own overhead:** a centred damage list under a top boss bar covered the player's overhead health on all four touch presets tried, about 27-35% of the screen height down at default zoom, and only a multi-row test showed it: one row had looked fine. Hung from the bar's left end, its right edge 0.145 of the screen height from the centre, it cleared the health bar on 705 x 338, 666 x 374, 959 x 599, 1179 x 818 and 1365 x 768. Test HUD lists full, over a character with its overhead.
- **"Leaderboard" in a request means Roblox's player list first:** a request to put a stat first in the leaderboard so players rank by it was built as the first in-world leaderboard board (a new ordered store and four rebuilt shrines); the owner meant the player list from `leaderstats` and asked for a revert. Confirm when a game also has world boards. The stat was already created first, but a `leaderstats` folder parented whole can replicate its children in any order: pin the order with a `BoolValue` named `IsPrimary` under the stat (the first column, which ranks the list) and a `NumberValue` named `Priority` (higher shows earlier): the client then showed the stat with `IsPrimary` and `Priority` 3, the others 2 and 1.
