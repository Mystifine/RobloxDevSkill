# UI: fight controls per device

On-screen attack and skill controls for touch, mouse and keyboard, and gamepad, beside [ui.md](ui.md), whose Mobile first rules apply.

- **Auto-face the nearest target on touch and gamepad** *(default for action games with auto-aimed skills)*: an owner wanted mobile and console players always turned to the closest enemy, lock on or off, so held attacks and skills hit without aiming. One render-step function owns `Humanoid.AutoRotate` (off while it has a target or the lock, given back once neither applies, never while training or in a cutscene), so it and shift lock never fight; out of reach, the lock's camera facing (165 degrees) or Roblox's own turning holds. A mouse keeps free turning. Turn by yaw angle: [gameplay.md](../code/gameplay.md#npc-movement).

## Touch
- **Know the jump button's rectangle and keep every control off it:** the PlayerModule's `TouchJump` draws a 70 px square whose left edge is 95 px in from the screen's right edge and whose bottom is 20 px up, when the screen's short side is 500 px or less; otherwise 120 px, 170 px in, 90 px up. A big attack button (112 units at 96, 112 from the corner) covered it on a phone, and the user reported the attack sitting over another button. Compute that rectangle per screen and check the layout against it: placing the attack left of it, with the skills on an arc round its upper left, cleared it.
- **The default fight layout is one ring centred on the jump button, the attack first** *(default)*: the attack straight left of the jump button, level with its centre (any lower left a phone's 390 px-high screen), then each skill turned up the ring by the chord that its own radius, the previous button's radius and the clearance need. It replaced skills on an arc round a big attack button; the user wanted the attack bottom left and the other buttons round the jump button, as in a mobile action game.
- **Space neighbouring round buttons for their outlines:** at 45 degrees apart on a reach of radii + 12 units they kept 6 units, which their 3-4 px outlines closed (UIBounds on the preview); 48 degrees and radii + 22 units gave about 19 units (11 px on a phone).
- **Size a ring so its last step ends by straight above its hub, and test it at the largest UI scale:** on a 175-unit ring the fourth button landed at 74.5 degrees, past vertical, and at Studio's 1920 x 1080 viewport (UI scale 1.42, a tablet by the 500 px rule) its right edge sat 13 px off the screen. The phone preview (a 0.6-scale layout drawn at the real scale) and a spec over 844 x 390 and 1024 x 768 both missed it; a spec over five screens up to 1920 x 1022 at 1.42 caught it. A 200-unit ring ends at 88 degrees, every button 32-70 px inside the right edge on all five. Check touch layouts from 844 x 390 at the smallest UI scale to 1920 x 1022 at the largest, edges included.
- **Place an extra touch button by measured clearance on phone and tablet sizes:** a shift-lock button set just above the ring's top button landed inside the offers column on a 1024 x 768 tablet, where that column sits at 0.43 of the screen's height, found by working the layout maths for each device. Moved into the ring's hollow, 120 design units up and left of the jump button, it kept at least 12 units clear of every button on five screen sizes. Check a new button against every right-side column on phone and tablet sizes, and pin its spot with a layout spec.
- **UIBounds measures boxes, so it flags round buttons on a diagonal:** two whose centres were 122 units apart (radii 60 and 40: 22 units clear) were reported as touching, because their boxes overlap. A spec computing circle gaps (centre distance minus both radii) confirmed the clearance.
- **A HUD side column stays on its edge round the touch controls:** an earlier pass dodged the touch ring on short phones by stepping the offer column left of it and hiding it during boss fights, and the user rejected it and asked for it back on the right. The replacement kept the right edge (6-10 px in) on Galaxy A06, iPhone 16 Pro, iPad 10th gen and iPad Pro 13, with 9.7-60 px clearance to the nearest button. Resolve an overlap by lifting the column, then shrinking its art, then scaling the column, never by moving it across the screen: a first shrink of the whole card clipped its caption ("STARTER PACK") on the A06, while shrinking only the artwork frame (its own `UIScale`, the caption anchored to the bottom) kept the text full size.

## Mouse and keyboard
- **Skills are rounded-square tiles with a key badge and the skill's name** *(default)*, not circles: an owner sent back circular skill icons on PC for tiles like their reference's, coloured, the key on a badge in a corner and the name across the foot. Touch keeps its round buttons on the ring.

## Gamepad
- **Keep the game's bindings off the platform's buttons:** A jumps, X triggers `ProximityPrompt`s and Start opens Roblox's menu. One layout put the attack and skills on RT, LB, RB and B, with Select moving into the HUD's menu, and drew each button from the platform's own picture (`UserInputService:GetImageForKeyCode`).
- **Switch off the engine's auto-select before binding Select to a HUD menu:** a `ContextActionService` binding on `ButtonSelect` that selects the left menu left the selection on the top bar's first button, since `GuiService.AutoSelectGuiEnabled` (on by default) acted first. With it off and the binding at high priority, Select put the selection on the menu's first tile. Bumpers can be sent through the keyboard tool ([play-input.md](../code/play-input.md), Gamepad): LB cast the bound skill (the stat it pays rose from 0 to 2).
