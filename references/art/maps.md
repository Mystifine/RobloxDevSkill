# Maps, bases and buildings

For maps built with parts, map expansions, bases, plot structures and buildings, beside [modeling.md](modeling.md), whose z-fighting rules and ZFight audit apply to every build.

- **Choose a build's colours under the game's runtime lighting** ([environment.md](environment.md), Lighting): a raised exposure washes Plastic mid tones and shifts Neon hues.
- **Fix the big surfaces before adding props** *(default)*: the sky, the borders, the walk surfaces and the light sources, then props. On a voxel map the default sky, bare border walls and a flat path were the biggest gaps to the references; a painted skybox, tile-capped walls with posts and a running-bond tile path with kerbs and lanterns changed the read more than more decor would, while the user was asking for half the decor.
- **A combat map dresses its sides and keeps its middle open** *(default)*: landmarks toward the walls, scatter only outside an open middle band, starting sparse (about a dozen props per map) and letting the landmarks carry each map's identity. Asked twice to halve a generated arena's decor, the user wanted what remained along the edges; the second pass took the props from 828 to 418 and those on the centre line from 41 to 27, the rest being the path's own centrepieces.
- **Check each structure from where players approach, in Play,** since Edit captures can skip SurfaceGuis: its sign, its centerpiece and any floating feature stay clear of the posts, beams, lanterns and gates in front of them, and bright materials and Neon trims are toned for the scene's bloom. A hanging sign hid its building's centerpiece and a lantern hung in front of the sign, two inner posts hid a stall sign's first and last letters, a gate beam hid an altar's floating card, and white marble with Neon trims bloomed to white at dusk; the ZFight audit and Edit captures showed none of it. Look from close at head height too: from 25 studs a ridge beam and a gold finial hid the lower line of two building labels (BillboardGuis without `AlwaysOnTop`) that top-down Edit captures showed clean, until the labels rose 2.2 and 3.4 studs over the tallest front piece. A label without `AlwaysOnTop` hides behind any geometry, its own prop's included: a booth's 8-stud label centred 16.4 studs up (its bottom at 12.4) lost its last line behind the booth's own front pillar caps (their tops at 13.5) from a camera about 10 studs up, and centred at 18.6 it cleared them; another prop's geometry (a portal's horn) clipped a label the same way until the booth moved 2 studs inward.
- **A label over a prop** is a BillboardGui with `StudsOffsetWorldSpace` on an upright anchor part: [runtime.md](../ui/runtime.md).
- **Labels of props near each other are checked together from the spawn's follow camera, in Play** (billboards show only in Play, so Edit captures miss the clash): a feedback box set 9 studs behind a chest and turned 5 degrees outward put its billboard (10 x 3.6 studs, 11.2 studs up) across the chest's (12 x 4 studs, 11 up). Directly behind it, with its label raised to 16 studs, the two stacked cleanly. Separate by height when one stands behind another.
- **Measure clearance between placed pieces by their real outline, not bounding boxes:** an axis-aligned box check put a rotated portal mesh 0.74 studs into a stair step and a round plinth 1.3 studs into a post box; downward rays on a 0.5-stud grid, filtered to `Include` the mesh alone, traced its real ground outline 5.0 studs clear of the stairs, and the disc against the box's foot 1.7 studs clear. Use boxes only as a first pass; meshes, cylinders and rotated parts get raycast outlines (or `GetPartsInPart`).
- **A hub puts the stations players use nearest the spawn, and its paid displays set back** *(default)*: an owner had the reward chest and the world portal moved from about 66 studs from the spawn to about 40 and 44, just outside the plaza ring, and the limited-offer booths moved out and back (x from 16 to 20 studs, z from -66 to -74), showing between the chest and the portal, visible but not aggressive; the chest's large Neon floor disc went as clutter.
- **A build cloned from another model keeps only the prompts and GUIs the new design uses:** pads cloned from an old game's pad carried an enabled ProximityPrompt ("Buy Slot / Purchase") and two disabled BillboardGuis no code handled, and the prompt showed on all 96 pads. To find strays, list the place's ProximityPrompts grouped by `ObjectText | ActionText` in one call.
- **Props on a generated map live in their own re-runnable scripts,** run after its builder and named in the builder's header: a builder that rebuilds its folder erases whatever was placed by hand.
- **Rebuilding part of a built map in Edit** from the project's Rojo-synced builder modules: clone them into a temporary folder and run in one synchronous call, never rename the synced modules to swap in copies, which once lost 21 of them ([studio-testing.md](../code/studio-testing.md#studio-mcp-calls)).
