# VFX from a supplied pack

For effects built from a pack's templates, beside [vfx.md](vfx.md); attacks also follow [hit-vfx.md](hit-vfx.md).

- **Survey an unnamed pack in rows:** a dark wall and plate, one row of templates at a time, each emitted and frozen at its own peak (0.6 x its largest `EmitDelay` + 0.2 s) and named by its place in the row, since labels did not render; group duplicates by shared texture sets. 96 unnamed templates took about 17 captures of four.
- **Pack emitters keep their designed facing:** read each emitter's `EmissionDirection` with its parent's orientation. A pack effect may fire out of one face by design (a wind ring's 13 emitters all left the part's Front face, so set to a character's root it rushed the way the character faced): rotate the carrier so that face points where the effect should travel, and keep the rotation the pack gave it (never overwrite it with `CFrame.identity`). Reference unnamed carriers by texture id.
- **Tune against the game's own lighting, not the pack's preview plate:** look at an effect again once tinted (one burst's rocks drew as black splats and its leaf shards as blue crystals). Slashes and crescents tuned on dark previews nearly vanished on a sunny cartoon map; 1.6 times the `Brightness` on emitters with `LightEmission` 0.5 or more, hot cores kept tinted instead of white, made them read without a white wash.
- **Cap a pack effect's life to its cast when playing it,** not by destroying it: scale each emitter's `Lifetime`, `EmitDelay` and `EmitDuration` at play time so everything ends by the hit plus a short tail *(default: an attack about its hit + 0.4 s, a skill about its last hit + 0.85 s)*, which keeps the fade curves. Curated templates kept smoke, ember and shard layers alive 2 to 4.4 s, long after a quick attack's hitbox; capped, a basic attack's particles fell from up to 3.5 s to 0.45 s.
