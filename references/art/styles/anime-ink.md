# Anime ink

Use only for the contours, painted shading and forms selected by the [visual-style dispatcher](../visual-style.md). This is not the default for character skins and does not prescribe a skeleton or map count.

- **Painted, not rendered:** saturated local colors with two or three tones and soft painted edges. Metal can be yellow paint with an ochre shadow, panel lines and a few glints when that matches the reference. Painted light is intentional in this treatment; do not apply the realistic-albedo rule blindly. Tune metallic response under the project's lighting so reflections do not erase the paint; a low metalness such as 0 to 0.3 is a stylistic starting point, not a physical material specification.
- **Smooth, tapered ink:** near-black strokes swell slowly and taper along rims, hems, seams and material borders. Follow a contour as one curve, not mesh edge by mesh edge. Abrupt width changes and strokes thresholded directly from ambient occlusion look rough. About 0.02 studs was useful for one character set; match the actual reference and viewing size.
- **Folds carry detail:** a stroke thickens at its pinch, runs out to a point and has soft shadow and light beside it. Fan folds from gathering points; drapes hang in long verticals and sleeves wrinkle across. Random strokes read as scratches. `kit.DRAPE`, `kit.WRINKLE` and `Pieces.ink` can help, but place folds according to construction and keep them off surfaces that should be smooth. Dense scratch-like marks can also cause upload problems ([no-gore.md](../../safety/no-gore.md)).
- **Draw small detail:** stitching, belt holes and muscle definition often belong in the texture. Use modeled plates, fur clumps, hems and garment volume where they establish form. Close wraps over the hands and soles where the design covers them; check moving joints ([rigs.md](../../rigs/rigs.md)).
- **Spend texels where seen:** resolution alone does not guarantee sharp lines. One atlas covering an entire character can leave the chest blurry. A reference build needed about 100 pixels per stud there; inspect at game size and split into regions only when that improves the result within budget. Avoid wasting atlas space on hollow islands.

`scripts/skin_kit.py` at the skill root provides `kit.paint`: painted tones, smooth ink and folds, plus relief, roughness and metalness bakes. It produces four maps at its requested `size`; use it when that treatment fits, and upload/apply only the maps needed. A hand-painted color texture is also valid. PBR is optional and compatible with this style; see [material selection](../modeling.md#materials-and-textures).

For a model-bound face or emblem, carry over its actual line weight and shading. A useful brief when those characteristics match is: "Near-black brush contours that swell and taper; flat local colors and one soft shadow tone." Judge at the intended display size: lines clean at 512 px may vanish on a head, so inspect around 128 px beside the model. Do not add contours or remove gradients when the reference calls for another treatment.
