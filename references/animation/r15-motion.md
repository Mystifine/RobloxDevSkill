# Realistic R15 motion from code

[scripts/r15_motion](../../scripts/r15_motion/r15_rig.py) needs no plugin, where a Motor6D rig's `.rbxanim` route needs Cautioned's Studio plugin, which no MCP can drive ([blender.md](blender.md)). `pipeline.py dump` reads the real R15 through Studio MCP (`C0`, `C1`, sizes and meshes through `EditableMesh`, all but the head), Blender builds the armature from that, a clip is sparse key poses (`example_clips.py`), and `pipeline.py import` writes KeyframeSequences back, audits them and round-trips within 0.009 studs. The headers say how to run each.

- **Sparse keys, then world-space springs, then exact IK:** each frame the keys give targets, each segment's spring follows its parent's result (overlap, follow-through and settle for free), and two-bone IK puts the feet where their tracks say, so a planted foot does not move at all. 39 clips baked in seconds and passed the inspector.
- **Scan for channels that drift:** a pose that leaves out a channel lets it run on from its last key: an arm moved for 10 s and a snap fired 0.9 s early, and the inspector passed them. The bake's `timing` report lists long segments with a large move.
- **Reach for another body with IK, not a direction:** aim at a point beyond reach on the target (its chest), in the root's space, so the arm points straight at it. Arm directions set in the chest's space of a 60° lunge came out 38° too high, then 1.2 studs to the side; IK to the target landed within 1-4° at every key.
- **A shoulder slides:** an R15 shoulder turns about the arm's inner face, so a raised arm swings over the head. A small joint translation, lifting and sliding the shoulder out with the arm's elevation, clears it (`girdle`).
- **Gait from leg length:** legs of 1.9 studs really walk at about 4 studs a second; 8 asked for 5.4 studs of stance, and 16 is a sprint needing a quarter-cycle stance and a flight phase. Animate time-warps walk and run independently, so they drift apart in a blend: speeds proportional to their lengths keep them in phase.
- **A body turning over with IK feet:** the straight way from above the hips to below them runs through the hip joint, so the legs flip by up to 178° in a frame, and delaying the feet or biasing the pole did not cure it. Move each ankle in two segments through a point behind the hips, turning the way the body turns (`swing_over`).
- **Blender FBX bones arrive turned 180° about their length:** local positions come in with X and Z negated, so a pose authored as Euler about the Blender bone's axes needs `FLIP * R * FLIP^-1` as `Bone.Transform` (FLIP = 180° about Y).
