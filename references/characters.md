# Characters (all-ages safety)

Applies to every character that could read as female, humanoid or creature, in 2D (icons, portraits, thumbnails) and 3D (models, textures, renders). Moderation can deactivate the owner's account, not just remove the asset. Female designs are fine under this filter:

1. **Flat chest** in silhouette, outlines, contour lines, shading and highlights: the chest piece is one flat plate or panel with no rounded volume. Straight torso: no pinched waist or hourglass.
2. **Covered, no cleavage.** Opaque armor or clothing covers the whole chest and at least 70% of the torso (front, shoulders to hips, or to where a tail, stem or flower begins). Its top edge, or the point of a V-neck, sits at the armpit line or higher, so no cleavage line, inner curve or bust shading shows. The neck, shoulders and a strip of upper chest may stay bare.
3. **The chest piece cannot pass for skin:** a clearly different color (metal, bark, cloth) or a much darker or lighter value, with visible texture and edges. Never the skin's own color, and on human skin never a flesh tone. Other pieces may echo the body's palette when they read as material (scales, plates or leaves with edges or trim).
4. **Never:** bra or bikini tops (two cups), bare midriff, paired round shapes on the chest, seductive poses or expressions (half-lidded eyes, pout, tongue out).

## Design the cover in character

Build the cover from the character's own materials and palette, shaped like armor or clothing it would wear: its jewelry metal as a breastplate, its scales, bark, leaves or petals as panels. It should look designed, not added. Avoid bulky collars, boxy tunics, robes or colors foreign to the character unless the design calls for them, since they change who it is.

To fix existing art, make a precise edit of that image: change only the torso cover, and an expression that breaks rule 4. Keep the pose, identity, palette, line style and outline.

## 2D prompts

Lead with the garment and keep negatives short: "[Character] wears [its own material as armor, for example a single flat gold breastplate and a dark scale panel with gold trim] over the chest and waist down to the [hips / tail / stem]. The breastplate's top edge sits at the armpit line: no cleavage, a flat chest, a straight torso." For an edit, add: "Change only the torso; keep everything else, including the outline."

## 3D

Model the chest flat: no bust geometry, and no bust shading in the normal or color maps. The chest piece is its own geometry or material. Renders of the model must pass the same rules.

## Review before any upload

View each image or render at full size and at 128 px. It fails if:
- the torso would read as nude or curvy with the body recolored to a human skin tone;
- the chest piece could pass for skin;
- any cleavage line, inner curve or bust shading shows, or less than 70% of the torso is covered;
- the cover looks pasted on (a foreign color, a bulky shape), or the outline differs from the rest of the set.

Fix the art first. Never upload to test moderation.
