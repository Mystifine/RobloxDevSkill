# Characters (all-ages safety)

Applies to every character that could read as female, humanoid or creature, in 2D (icons, portraits, thumbnails) and 3D (models, textures, renders). Moderation can deactivate the owner's account, not just remove the asset. Female designs are fine under this strict filter:

1. **Flat chest** in silhouette, outlines, contour lines, shading and highlights. Straight torso: no pinched waist or hourglass.
2. **Covered torso.** Opaque clothing or armor covers at least 70% of the torso (front, shoulders to hips, or to where a tail, stem or flower begins) and all of the chest below a V-neck. The V ends above the bust line, with no cleavage. Design so only the neck and that V show.
3. **The cover contrasts with the skin** in hue and value and is never a flesh tone (beige, peach, pink, tan, brown). The body's own color counts as skin, so leaves on a green plant body or teal scales on a mint naga fail. Give the cover garment edges: a collar, hem, trim or plates.
4. **Never:** bra, bikini, bandeau or tube tops; bare midriff; paired round shapes on the chest (gems, spots, buttons); seductive poses or expressions (half-lidded eyes, pout, tongue out).

## 2D prompts

Lead with the garment and keep negatives short: "[Character] wears an opaque [garment] in [color unlike the skin], from a high collar or shallow V-neck over both shoulders, the whole flat chest and the waist, down to the [hips / tail / stem]. Straight, untapered torso. No cleavage, bust shape or chest contour lines."

## 3D

Model the chest flat: no bust geometry, and no bust shading in the normal or color maps. The cover is its own geometry or material in the contrasting color. Renders of the model must pass the same rules.

## Review before any upload

View each image or render at full size and at 128 px. It fails if:
- the torso would read as nude or curvy with the body recolored to a human skin tone;
- the cover could pass for skin, by its color or by hugging the body's shape;
- skin shows below the V, or less than 70% of the torso is covered.

Fix the art first (more cover, a flatter chest, a contrasting color). Never upload to test moderation.
