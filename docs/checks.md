Folded meshes, after a user's screenshot of a flickering belt buckle on a skin: a crescent outline whose inner arc ran past the outer circle crossed itself, so its cap folded over itself (on R15, 19 flipped triangles, 24 overlapping pairs and four times the crescent's area, a filled hollow). Every audit passed it: blender-asset-qa skips triangles that share a vertex, and ZFight does not read MeshParts, so its 0 said nothing about a skin. The bevel was not the cause: the outline folded with `b=0`, while sharp-tipped crescents and the HeroSkins pendant did not. Now `kit.prism` refuses a crossing outline, `rr.folds` measures a mesh's visible overlap with itself, both exports refuse 0.002 studs² or more, and `ZFight.audit` counts the MeshParts and unions it skipped. The limit comes from measuring 14 shipped assets and the original buckle: buried creases (up to 72 pairs) do not count, and visible overlap was 0.212 on the buckle, 0.017 to 0.028 on DemonGirl's and DragonGirl's legs and feet, and at most 0.00065 elsewhere. The DragonGirl hit was a real defect, a black wedge in a toe claw. All Blender tests (7 new) and the Python tests passed, Selene is clean, the 23 `prism` calls in the HeroSkins designs still build, and in Studio `ZFight.audit` reported 8 MeshParts unread on a dressed R6, 32 on an R15 and no note on plain Parts.

Accessory types and joints, after the same reference was rebuilt on the Blender path and both generated rigs came out bald. Four black messy hairs from the catalog's hair category were all FaceAccessory assets (type 42), not HairAccessory (41). The rigs.md recipe declared them as `Hair`, and `CreateHumanoidModelFromDescription` dropped each one without an error, on R6 and R15. Declared as `Face`, or set through `desc.FaceAccessory`, each came through welded, about 35 studs off its seat, which `seat` fixes. `Humanoid:AddAccessory` joins the handle 12 to 18 frames after the call, in Workspace and in ServerStorage alike. On R6 the joint is a Weld named AccessoryWeld. On R15 it is a RigidConstraint named AccessoryRigidConstraint, which RigAudit looked for under the wrong name: it called a correctly worn R15 hair `unwelded`, and `seat` skipped it.
- **Fixed:** RigAudit reads both joints, and rigs.md has each accessory entered with its asset's own type, read from `GetProductInfo`.
- **Tests:** `RigAudit.spec.luau` passed 26 checks twice, including 4 new ones: the joint each rig gets, and the audit and `seat` finding it. Selene is clean.
- **Not staged:** an accessory jointed but off its seat. In Edit a joint snaps its handle in whenever it is made or re-parented, and setting a jointed handle's CFrame moves the whole assembly, so only the generator's output shows that state.
- **The character itself** (DaggerHunter) came out of this run: two skins and two daggers from Blender, 37 uploads approved, RigAudit and RigSkin at 0 errors on both rigs, and a walk with the skin 0.000 studs from its joints.
